About

A speaker on resilience, relationships that sell and teams that win

Ben Rodriguez Rolam is Vice President of Field Sales, Events, and Training, Americas at Sol de Janeiro. The youngest of twelve children of Mexican farmworkers, he arrived in Chico, California at eleven — sin inglés. He studied at Academy of Art University in San Francisco, then managed the Erno Laszlo counter at Nordstrom, growing it from $900 to $12,000 in eight months. He now leads field sales, events, and training across the Americas from Los Angeles.

Story

Three beats from the journey

01

Mexico to Chico

Born and raised in Mexico until age 11. He moved to Chico, California, the son of farmworkers and the youngest of 12, speaking no English.

02

The $900 counter

In 2007, at Nordstrom, he managed the Erno Laszlo counter and grew it from $900 to $12,000 in eight months.

03

From the Floor to VP

Nearly twenty years in prestige beauty — training 50-plus accounts, a decade at Algenist, then Sol de Janeiro — named Vice President, Field Sales, Events, and Training, Americas, in April 2026. Based in Los Angeles.
