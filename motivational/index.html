Why Ben

A speaker who started where many students start

Ben was born and raised in Mexico until age 11. He moved to Chico, California, the son of farmworkers and the youngest of twelve, speaking no English. He studied at Academy of Art University in San Francisco, and today he is Vice President of Field Sales, Events, and Training, Americas at Sol de Janeiro.

Ben talks with Latino students and community groups about belonging, resilience, and finding your voice when the room does not feel built for you, and about what becomes possible when you keep showing up.

Talks

Three motivational talks

01

From the Pueblo to the Podium

Del pueblo al podio · signature keynote · 30–45 minutes. Ben’s journey from an 11-year-old arriving in Chico with no English to leading field teams across the Americas—and what it can show students about courage, belonging, and not letting anyone else define their limits.

02

Sin inglés

Sin inglés · assembly or classroom talk. What it is like to start over in a new country and a new language, and how students can find their voice when everything feels unfamiliar.

03

Start Where You Are

Empieza donde estás · career-readiness talk. In 2007, Ben took the Erno Laszlo counter at Nordstrom from $900 to $12,000 in eight months. A talk about effort, relationships, and learning on the job, for students thinking about internships and early careers.
