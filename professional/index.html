Why Ben

A leader who built his career from the floor up

Ben is Vice President of Field Sales, Events, and Training, Americas at Sol de Janeiro. He joined Sol de Janeiro in 2023, after about a decade at Algenist, and was named VP in April 2026. He also trained 50-plus accounts at HTI, including Sephora, Nordstrom, and Neiman Marcus. In 2007 he grew the Erno Laszlo counter at Nordstrom from $900 to $12,000 in eight months.

He is the youngest of twelve children of Mexican farmworkers and arrived in Chico, California at eleven with no English. Ben speaks to Latino employees, and the leaders who want to grow them, about resilience, relationships that sell, and teams that win.
